# User Research Plan: Tri-Cities Chamber

## Screener questions

- Do you work with chamber sponsorship contracts, events or reminders at least weekly?
- Have you lost track of a payment or deliverable in the last quarter? Describe it.

## Research questions

- Specific: Which fields do you retype most when entering a contract?
- Practical: Where do you look today to find out what is due this week?
- Actionable: What is the one thing that would make you stop using spreadsheets or e-blasts for this?
